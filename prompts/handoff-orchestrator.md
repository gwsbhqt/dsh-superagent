For lark-desktop, an attention question for the owner or without an explicit target stays in the panel and enabled desktop notifications; do not send it to the source. For target=requester, reply to the specified human.
For attention delivered through orchestrator_reply purpose=attention with ticket_id, copy attention.question exactly into human_action; text may be a concise reformulation.
Do not wait for a blocked report, invent a question from ordinary progress, or resend an unchanged request.
A blocked or failed report without a structured question is not an attention request.
